About Our College

Dedicated to Excellence in Education

Welcome to GCCA

Godavari College of Computer Applications stands as a beacon of educational excellence, committed to nurturing talented minds and transforming them into industry-ready professionals.

Our institution has consistently delivered top-notch education with a focus on practical knowledge, research, and innovation. We believe in holistic development of our students, combining academic rigor with real-world application.

  • Affiliated with KBCNMU
  • Modern Infrastructure
  • Experienced Faculty
  • Industry Partnerships

Our Mission

To provide world-class education in computer applications and related fields, fostering innovation, critical thinking, and social responsibility among our students.

Our Vision

To emerge as a leading institution of excellence in computer applications education, recognized nationally and internationally for our contributions to technology and society.

Our Values

Integrity, excellence, innovation, and inclusivity guide every decision we make, ensuring our students grow into ethical and accomplished professionals.

Dr. Nillima Chaudhary

Admission Process

Simple and Transparent Steps

1

Online Registration

Fill out the online registration form with your basic details. Registration is free and takes only 5 minutes.

2

Document Verification

Submit your educational certificates, ID proofs, and other required documents for verification.

3

Entrance Test / Counseling

Appear for entrance examination (if applicable) or counseling session with our admission team.

4

Fee Payment

Pay the first installment fee through online portal or at college office. Multiple payment options available.

5

Admission Confirmation

Receive admission confirmation letter and instructions for reporting to campus. Welcome to GCCA family!
